# Reports

Ask for something longer than a chat answer, such as "compare these three laptops", "write up what changed in this project this month", or "chart my meetings per week", and Ensō can write a **report**: a document with headings, tables and charts that opens in a new tab.

## Charts

Reports can include bar and line charts. Ensō builds them from data it has gathered in the conversation: from pages, your tools or your history. Each chart has a title, labelled axes and a legend.

## Finding them again

Every report is saved and listed under **Reports** in the full-page app, newest first, with a link back to the conversation that made it. Reports open in an ordinary tab, so you can print them or save them as PDF from the browser's menu.

## Where they are stored

Reports are files on your Mac, in `~/Library/Application Support/zen-chat/reports/`. They are never uploaded, except as encrypted items if you turn on [sync](/docs/sync).
