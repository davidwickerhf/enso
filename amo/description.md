Ensō puts Claude in your browser. It is made for Zen (and runs in Firefox 139 or later), on macOS.

**Sidebar chat.** Ask about the page you're on, mention other tabs with @, attach files or screenshots. Claude can read the page and, when you ask, click, type and fill forms for you. Actions it starts on its own ask first, and sending, paying or deleting always asks.

**A calm new tab.** A short daily brief and widgets for your calendar, mail, tasks (Commonfold, monday.com and other task apps), GitHub, quick links and a note. A search box with your search engine's suggestions and your history.

**Your browser as a tool for agents.** Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other MCP clients can read and act in your signed-in Zen through Ensō, with every action shown on the page and in an activity log, and buttons to pause or stop an agent. You can also send a screenshot of a tab to an agent or to T3 Code with ⌘⇧2.

**It needs a helper app.** Ensō talks to a small local helper (Node.js, macOS) over native messaging; the helper runs Claude through Claude Code on your own Claude plan, or with your Anthropic API key. After installing the add-on, Ensō shows the one-line install command (from enso.wicker.life/install.sh) and finishes setup itself.

**Privacy.** Your conversations, memory and settings stay on your Mac. What you ask goes to Anthropic, your AI provider; nothing goes through a server of ours, and there is no analytics or telemetry. Banking, payment, password-manager, health, tax and government sites are never read or acted on. The privacy policy below lists exactly what goes where.
